Learning & experience design

I make complex things usable.

I take material that’s dense, flat, or overwhelming and make it navigable — removing friction at exactly the points where people tend to get stuck. That work shows up as eLearning, performance support, planning, and strategy, but the through-line is always the same: clarity people can move through.

What that looks like in practice

Different formats, one job: reduce the effort it takes for a person to understand and act.

Learning experiences

Scenario-based, gamified, reflective, and utility builds — designed around where learners actually stumble, not just what needs to be covered.

Performance support

Job aids and in-the-moment tools that put the right guidance at the point of need, so people don’t have to hold everything in their head.

Planning & strategy

Project plans, learning strategy, and pitches that turn a tangled problem into a path a team can actually follow.

A little about me

I’m an instructional designer and eLearning developer who works with clients to make learning and information genuinely usable. I build in code as comfortably as in authoring tools, and I care about getting the details right — including accessibility, which is built into how I work rather than bolted on at the end.

I’m always open to new freelance and contract work. Get in touch if you’ve got material that needs to become clear.
